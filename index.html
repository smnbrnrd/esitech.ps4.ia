Description and materials

The materials below are provided as part of the “Machine Learning” course for fourth-year students in the Physics for Health department at ESITECH school of engineering @ University of Rouen Normandy, France.

This course is an introduction to the key techniques that form the core of modern AI methods today. It is not intended to provide the knowledge necessary to master these techniques, but rather to provide the understanding needed to use them effectively, and it also serves as a good starting point for further study.

This courses is divided into 6 lectures:

Lecture Title Links
1 Fundamentals of Machine Learning Introduction to the core concepts of machine learning (supervised learning, loss and risk minimization, overfitting) Slides PDF
2 Linear models Introduction to linear regression and classification (Least Squares, Ridge and Lasso regression, Logistic regression) Slides PDF
3 Deep neural networks Introduction to deep neural networks with an emphasis on the practical aspects of training Slides PDF
4 Deep learning for images Introduction to deep learning techniques for image analysis and computer vision (Convolutional Neural Networks) Slides PDF
5 Deep learning for sequences Introduction to deep learning techniques for sequence analysis (Recurrent Neural Networks and Transformers) Slides PDF
6 Deep learning for physics Introduction to deep learning techniques for physics applications (Physics-Informed Neural Networks, Neural Operators) Slides PDF
